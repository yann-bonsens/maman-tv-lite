# Individual Contributor Licence Agreement

## In plain language, before the legal text

This project is GPL-3.0 and will stay GPL-3.0. The author may one day want to
sell a product built on it, and GPL-3.0 alone would not let him — so for **code**
contributions he asks for one extra permission, once.

What it means for you:

- **You keep the copyright in your contribution.** Nothing is transferred.
- **You keep the right to use your own work however you like**, including in your
  own commercial products.
- **Your contribution ships under GPL-3.0 like everything else**, so anyone
  receiving it gets the source and the same freedoms.
- **In addition**, you let the author include it in a commercially licensed
  product without further restriction.
- **None of this touches a fork.** Fork the project and your changes are yours,
  under GPL-3.0, with no agreement to sign and nothing owed to anybody.

**You do not need to sign anything** for documentation fixes, television
profiles, issue reports, or test cases. Only code.

If you would rather not sign, that is a legitimate choice — send the bug report
anyway, or keep your work in your fork. Both are useful.
