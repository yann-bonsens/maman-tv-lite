# Code of conduct

This project is about making a television usable by somebody who cannot use a
remote control. Keep that person in mind and the rest follows.

**Expected:** patience, plain language, and the assumption that the person you
are talking to is doing their best with the information they have. Many people
here are not programmers — they are somebody's son, daughter or carer, trying to
make a television work. Answer them as you would want your own family answered.

**Not accepted:** personal attacks, harassment, discrimination, condescension
about somebody's technical level, or contempt for the people this project is
built for — including jokes at the expense of elderly or disabled users.

**Scope:** issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and any other
space where this project is being worked on.

**Enforcement:** the maintainer will edit, close or delete anything that breaks
this, and will block anyone who repeats it. There is no committee; there is one
person reading. Report a problem privately through GitHub's *Report content*, or
by email if you know it.

This is deliberately short. It is not a legal document, it is a statement that
unpleasantness will be dealt with rather than debated.
